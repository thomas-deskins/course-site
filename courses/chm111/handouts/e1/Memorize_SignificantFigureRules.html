Significant Figures 1. Rules for Counting Significant Figures (sf or sig. figs) e All non-zero digits are significant. (e.g. 123 has 3 sf) ¢ Zeros between non-zero digits are significant. (e.g. 12304 has 5 sf) ¢ Leading zeros are not significant. (e.g. 0.0012 has 2 sf) ¢ Trailing zeros are significant only if a decimal point is present. (e.g. 120 has 2 sf; 120. has 3 sf; 120.30 has 5 sf) 2. Addition & Subtraction ¢ Round the result to the same decimal place as the least precise term. i.e. count the number of digits after the decimal place for each addend, subtrahend, and minuend. Keep the minimum number of digits after the decimal place in your answer. 12.11 +0.3 + 1.025 = 13.435 Toundto tenths, 13 4 3.
