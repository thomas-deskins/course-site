# Accessibility Statement

## Commitment

I strive to make course materials accessible to all students, regardless of ability or technology. My goal is to provide an inclusive learning environment where everyone can equally access, engage with, and benefit from course content.

## Standards

All course materials provided here follow the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA and current accessibility regulations ([28 CFR § 35.200](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-35.200 "Requirements for web and mobile accessibility")). These materials are designed to be compatible with a range of assistive technologies, including screen readers and keyboard-only navigation.

## What Is Already Accessible

I have taken the following steps to ensure course materials are consistently usable across a range of assistive technologies:

- Webpages use structured HTML with semantic elements (headings, lists, tables) to support screen readers and keyboard navigation.

- Documents are formatted with clear hierarchy, readable fonts, and sufficient color contrast for ease of reading.

- Navigation is consistent across pages to reduce cognitive load and improve orientation.

### PDF and Scanned Materials

- All scanned PDFs are accompanied by equivalent, accessible HTML versions.

- When possible, documents are provided in formats that support text selection, search, and screen reader access. 

## Known Limitations

Mathematical content may require tools such as MathJax or compatible screen readers.

## Requesting Assistance or Accommodations

If you encounter accessibility barriers or need materials in an alternative format, please contact me through Canvas.

To help me assist you, please include the following:

- Course name and number (e.g. PHY201)

- The specific file or page in question

- A description of the issue you encountered

- The format or accommodation you need
